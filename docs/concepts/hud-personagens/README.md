### Personagens

Use case: stylized-concept. Create an exceptionally beautiful professional game CHARACTER REDESIGN EXPLORATION BOARD for SAKURA BLITZ. Large wide landscape board on warm ivory paper. Title "SAKURA BLITZ / PERSONAGENS / COZY PS1". This board must help choose the game's future art direction; not an existing screenshot, not finalized game-ready assets. The brief is cozy PS1-style low-poly 3D with the exquisite handmade fantasy richness, whimsical clothing and painterly warm colors associated with Legend of Mana, but entirely original characters and costume designs.
Show THREE CLEARLY DIFFERENT production-feasible character directions in equal columns, all equally polished:
A "EXPLORADORES" — human young adult adventurers, expressive faces, head about one third of height, tiny angular boots and hands, cropped burgundy jacket over cream shirt, moss shorts, rich pink scarf, leather satchel, cherry blossom brooch, chestnut chunky sculpted hair. Adventurous and sporty rather than generic modern military.
B "GUARDIÕES DO JARDIM" — charming original small forest-folk adults with expressive faces and petal/leaf-shaped hood silhouettes, sage layered poncho, berry-red belt, practical wrapped boots, brass button and hand-painted embroidered seams, warm deep brown skin visible, friendly grounded proportions.
C "CORREIOS DAS NUVENS" — original whimsical courier adult hero, asymmetrical cozy teal flight jacket, ochre scarf, round goggles above eyebrow, pumpkin-red short hair, roomy cream trousers, satchel and flower pin, energetic athletic silhouette.
In EACH column show one LARGE full-body THREE-QUARTER FRONT low-poly 3D model concept and a smaller BACK view, plus one face closeup or small running pose detail. Distinct costumes, hairstyles and human-like body structures, not identical capsule jellybeans. Faces expressive with simple large carefully drawn eyes, nose and brows, warm appealing personalities, strong readable silhouettes and friendly competitive energy. Include a little original compact flower-painted blaster held safely lowered by explorer and a small checkpoint flag prop with courier; focus overwhelmingly on character design, not weapons. All clothing details use painted textures and large readable shapes; no jewelry noise, no guns aimed at viewer, no gore.
STYLE MUST SHOW ACTUAL LOW POLYGON FACETED GEOMETRY: angular hair clumps, broad planar clothing folds, mitten hands, chunky footwear, short articulated limbs that can run and jump. Soft hand-painted 128–256px-style diffuse texture, selective visible pixel texture at seams, rich restrained color variation, ambient occlusion painted in, no glossy plastic, no smooth high-end Pixar sculpture, no photorealism, no modern anime gacha rendering. Illustrative sophistication compatible with a lightweight mobile game. Lighting soft warm studio, clear cast shadow grounding each model.
BOTTOM show tiny silhouette lineup of the three characters to compare readability at small game scale, and six costume palette swatches. Very short Portuguese labels, elegant editorial board, balanced composition with whitespace, no stat sheet or long prose, no copied existing characters or franchise logos, no watermark. Make this genuinely rich and aesthetically distinctive, sophisticated cozy fantasy rather than generic blocky toy soldiers.
